Philosophy

Technology as a Mirror, Not an Authority

We believe technology has become exceptionally good at helping people understand the world around them. But when it comes to understanding ourselves, most digital experiences remain fragmented, generic, or overly clinical.

"We don't build technology to tell people who they are. We build technology that helps people discover more about who they are."

A person is more than their personality type. More than their zodiac sign. More than their habits. More than their past experiences. And more than the data generated by their devices.

Human beings are complex, constantly changing, and shaped by many different forces. Our products therefore bring together astrology, mindfulness, psychology frameworks, and restorative sleep into a unified daily mirror.
