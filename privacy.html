Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026 · Last Updated: September 6, 2026
Compliant with Apple App Store Guidelines, Google Play Developer Policies, GDPR & CCPA.

Our Privacy Pledge: At Night Pipers, we believe the inner world is sacred. Your journal entries, birth details, mood check-ins, and daily reflections belong exclusively to you. We do not sell your personal data, nor do we use your private thoughts to train public foundational AI models.

1. Overview & Applications Covered

This Privacy Policy explains how Night Pipers Inc. ("Night Pipers", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, encrypts, and protects your information across our mobile applications (including Mind Your Stars, future family of apps) and associated backend cloud services.

This document serves as the official Privacy Policy referenced in mobile app store listings and in-app redirection links.

2. Information We Collect

To provide personalized reflection, astrological analysis, and secure synchronisation across your devices, we collect the following categories of data:

Category Data Elements Purpose
Account & Authentication Email address, unique user ID, salted & hashed password, or tokenized credentials (Sign in with Apple / Google Sign-In). Secure user authentication, account recovery, and cross-device sync.
Astrological Profile Date of birth, exact time of birth, birth city/geographic coordinates. Mathematical calculation of planetary ephemeris, natal chart placements, and current transits in Mind Your Stars.
Daily User Reflections Daily mood logs, sleep quality & nighttime ritual notes, journal reflections, guided meditation responses. Stored securely to compile your personal history, reveal recurring emotional patterns, and display self-understanding trends.
Device & Diagnostics App version, device model, operating system version, crash reports. Maintaining app reliability, debugging performance, and preventing fraudulent activity.

3. Backend Storage & Security Architecture

Night Pipers utilizes enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure to store and manage your daily reflections and account data.

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between your mobile device and our backend servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).
  • Encryption at Rest: All stored personal identifiers, journal entries, and astrological data are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.
  • Zero Data Selling: We never sell, lease, or monetize your personal information or reflection entries to data brokers, ad networks, or third parties.

4. The Role of Artificial Intelligence & Reflection

Our proprietary reflection engine utilizes advanced AI models to connect themes across your astrological transits and daily reflections.

  • Ephemeral Processing: Prompts sent for contextual reflection analysis are processed in transient inference environments and are not permanently retained by LLM infrastructure providers.
  • No Model Training: Your private journal entries, dreams, emotional disclosures, and birth details are strictly barred from training public generative models.

5. User Rights, Data Export & Account Deletion

In accordance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v), Google Play User Data policies, the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), you have complete control over your data:

A. Self-Service In-App Deletion

You may delete your account and all associated data directly within any of our apps at any time:

  1. Open the app (e.g. Mind Your Stars).
  2. Navigate to Settings ➔ Account & Security.
  3. Tap Delete Account & Purge Data and confirm.

This action irreversibly erases your profile, authentication records, birth charts, journal logs, and history from our live databases within 72 hours.

B. Web & Email Erasure Request

If you have uninstalled the app or prefer to request deletion externally, email our Data Protection team at privacy@nightpipers.com with the subject line "Request for Account Deletion" from your registered email address. We will verify your identity and permanently delete all records within 30 days.

C. Data Portability & Export

You have the right to receive an export of your daily logs and astrological data in a standard JSON or CSV format. In-app export is available under Settings ➔ Privacy & Data Export.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with select, highly vetted infrastructure providers who adhere to strict data security standards:

  • Authentication Providers: Apple (Sign in with Apple) and Google (Google Identity Services) for tokenized, secure logins.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: AWS / Google Cloud Platform for hosting encrypted databases and serverless APIs.
  • App Store Payments: All premium subscriptions are processed directly through Apple StoreKit or Google Play In-App Billing; Night Pipers never sees or stores your credit card details.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not designed for or directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we learn that a child has provided us with personal data, we will take immediate steps to purge such data from our systems.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product enhancements, legal requirements, or evolving industry standards. If material changes are made, we will notify you through an in-app notice and update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
